Subject: [TOPIC] — trying something a bit different

Hi all,

We need to decide [TOPIC], and rather than one person
picking something and hoping for the best, I want to try
a process that actually surfaces what works for everyone
before we commit.

It takes about 10 minutes across three short steps spread
over [TIMEFRAME]. Here's what it involves.


================================================================
  THE IDEA
================================================================

Most group decisions fail in one of two ways: the loudest
voice wins, or we compromise so much that nobody's actually
happy with the outcome.

This process uses an AI tool to read everyone's views and
draft candidate consensus statements — things it thinks
the whole group could get behind. We then react to those,
critique them, and vote on a shortlist. The AI does the
synthesis work; all the actual input and judgment comes
from us.

It's based on research from Google DeepMind (they ran it
with 5,000+ people on contested political topics and it
outperformed human mediators at finding common ground).
We're using it to pick a venue, which feels like a lower
stakes proving ground.


================================================================
  THE THREE STEPS
================================================================

  STEP 1 — SHARE YOUR VIEW  (~5 minutes)
  ----------------------------------------
  One free-text question. Tell us what matters to you and
  — importantly — what your hard constraints are. Things
  that would make the option unworkable for you, not just
  less ideal.

  No word limit. Bullet points are fine. More honest =
  better output.

  I'll ask for this separately once everyone's in.


  STEP 2 — QUICK REACTION  (~3 minutes)
  ----------------------------------------
  After I've run everyone's responses through the system,
  I'll share a draft consensus statement it produced.

  All I'll ask is: what's missing or wrong with this?
  If you're happy with it, no action needed. If it's missed
  something important to you, a sentence or two saying what
  is enough.


  STEP 3 — VOTE  (~2 minutes)
  ----------------------------------------
  A shortlist of 5-6 refined statements. You rate each one:
  Agree / Pass / Disagree. Takes about two minutes.

  The results show us not just which statement "wins" but
  whether there are different opinion clusters in the group
  and which statements genuinely bridge them.


================================================================
  WHAT HAPPENS WITH YOUR RESPONSES
================================================================

Your free-text opinion goes directly into the AI as context
for generating the consensus statements. It's not summarised
or paraphrased — it's read as-is. The system is trying to
find formulations that reflect what everyone actually said,
not an average of it.

I can share the full output at any point if you want to see
what the system produced and why.
