================================================================
  THE IDEA
================================================================

Most group decisions go wrong in one of two ways: the
loudest voice wins, or we compromise so much that nobody's
happy. This process tries to fix both.

It uses an AI tool to read everyone's views and identify
where genuine agreement exists — and where it doesn't.
The AI does the synthesis work; all the actual input and
judgment comes from us.

Your responses feed two things running in parallel:

  QUICK AI READ (immediate)
  As soon as opinions are in, the AI produces a draft
  consensus statement — its best read of where the group
  could land. I'll share this for a sense-check. It's
  useful for planning and for catching anything the AI
  obviously missed.

  DEMOCRATIC VOTE (takes about a week)
  Your opinions are broken into short atomic statements —
  one idea each. You vote Agree / Pass / Disagree on each.
  The results show which statements genuinely bridge
  everyone, which divide opinion, and where the real
  tension is.

Both feed a short workshop where we finalise the decision
together. The AI has done the groundwork; we do the
deciding.

It's based on research from Google DeepMind (tested with
5,000+ people on contested political topics) and Polis
(used by governments for large-scale deliberation).


================================================================
  YOUR THREE STEPS
================================================================

  STEP 1 — SHARE YOUR VIEW  (~5 minutes)
  ----------------------------------------
  One free-text question. What matters to you and what
  are your hard constraints — things that would make an
  option genuinely unworkable, not just less ideal.

  No word limit. Bullet points are fine.


  STEP 2 — QUICK REACTION  (~2 minutes, optional)
  ----------------------------------------
  I'll share the AI's draft statement for a sense-check.
  If it's missed something important, a sentence saying
  what. If it looks right, no reply needed.


  STEP 3 — VOTE  (~2 minutes)
  ----------------------------------------
  A list of short statements drawn from everyone's views.
  Rate each: Agree / Pass / Disagree.


================================================================
  WHAT HAPPENS WITH YOUR RESPONSES
================================================================

Your free-text opinion goes directly into the AI as
context. It isn't summarised or paraphrased — it's read
as-is. The system finds formulations that reflect what
everyone actually said, not an average of it.

I can share the full output at any point.
